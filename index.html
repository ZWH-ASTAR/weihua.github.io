Weihua Zheng

I am a Senior Research Engineer at A*STAR, Singapore. My research interests include natural language processing, multilingual large language models, machine translation, question answering, and agentic AI systems.

I am particularly interested in building multilingual and culturally-aware AI systems, evaluating large language models, and developing practical NLP technologies for real-world applications.
